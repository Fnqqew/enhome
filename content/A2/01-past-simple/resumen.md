# Pasado simple

El **pasado simple** sirve para contar cosas que pasaron y **terminaron** en un momento del pasado: *ayer, la semana pasada, en 2019, hace dos días*. En español cubre tanto "fui" como, muchas veces, "iba".

## Día 1 — Was y were

Es el pasado de **to be** (era, estaba, fui, estuve).

| Sujeto | Afirmativo | Negativo | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / he / she / it | was | wasn't | Was she...? |
| you / we / they | were | weren't | Were they...? |

- *The concert **was** amazing.* → El recital estuvo increíble.
- *We **weren't** at home.* → No estábamos en casa.
- ***Were** you busy? — Yes, I **was**.* → ¿Estabas ocupado? — Sí.

Expresiones de tiempo en pasado:

- **yesterday** (ayer), **last** night / week / year (anoche, la semana pasada, el año pasado)
- two days **ago** (hace dos días) — *ago* va **después** del período
- **in** 2020, **in** March

## Día 2 — Verbos regulares

Se agrega **-ed**, igual para todas las personas.

| Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| General: + ed | work → work**ed**, play → play**ed** |
| Termina en e: + d | like → like**d**, dance → dance**d** |
| Consonante + y → ied | study → stud**ied**, try → tr**ied** |
| Consonante-vocal-consonante (una sílaba): se duplica | stop → sto**pped**, plan → pla**nned** |

**Pronunciación** (muy importante para no sonar raro):

- Solo después de **t** o **d** se agrega una sílaba (/ɪd/): *want**ed***, *need**ed***.
- En el resto **no** se agrega sílaba: *worked* suena "workt", *played* suena "pleid".

## Día 3 — Verbos irregulares más usados

No siguen ninguna regla: hay que aprenderlos. Estos son los más frecuentes:

| Verbo | Pasado | Significado |
|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | ser, estar |
| have | had | tener |
| do | did | hacer |
| go | went | ir |
| get | got | conseguir, llegar |
| make | made | hacer, fabricar |
| see | saw | ver |
| come | came | venir |
| take | took | tomar, llevar |
| eat | ate | comer |
| buy | bought | comprar |
| think | thought | pensar |
| say | said | decir |
| tell | told | contar, decir |
| give | gave | dar |
| find | found | encontrar |

Tip: agrupalos por sonido — *buy/bought, bring/brought, think/thought*; *know/knew, grow/grew*.

## Día 4 — Negativo y preguntas con did

Igual que el presente simple usa *do/does*, el pasado usa **did** para todas las personas.

| | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Negativo | sujeto + **didn't** + verbo base | I **didn't go** out. |
| Pregunta | **Did** + sujeto + verbo base | **Did** you **call** her? |
| Respuesta corta | Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. | |

La regla de oro: **el pasado lo lleva *did***, así que el verbo principal vuelve a la forma base.

- *She didn't **go*** ✔️ — *She didn't went* ❌
- *Did you **see** it?* ✔️ — *Did you saw it?* ❌

Con palabras de pregunta: ***Where did** you go? **What did** you buy? **Why didn't** you come?*

**Excepción:** con *was/were* no se usa did: *Were you tired?* (no *Did you be tired?*).

## Día 5 — Contar lo que pasó

Para narrar, ordená los hechos con conectores:

**First** → **Then** → **After that** → **Later** → **Finally**

Y usá **when** para unir dos momentos: *When we arrived, it started to rain.*

> Last weekend was great. On Saturday I got up late and had breakfast in bed. Then I met my friends in the city center. We walked around and had lunch at a small restaurant. After that, we went to the cinema. When I got home, I was very tired, so I went to bed early. On Sunday I didn't do anything special.

## En pocas palabras

- **was / were** para el pasado de to be.
- Regulares: **-ed**. Irregulares: **de memoria** (*went, had, saw...*).
- Negativo y preguntas: **didn't / Did + verbo base**.
- Pistas: *yesterday, last..., ...ago, in 2020*.
