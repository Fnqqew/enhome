# Comparar y cuantificar

Esta semana aprendés a **comparar** (más alto, el mejor, tan caro como) y a hablar de **cantidades** (algo de, mucho, pocos).

## Día 1 — Comparativos

La forma depende de la **longitud del adjetivo**.

| Tipo de adjetivo | Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Corto (1 sílaba) | + **-er** + than | cheap → cheap**er** than |
| Termina en e | + **-r** | nice → nice**r** |
| Consonante-vocal-consonante | duplica + -er | big → bi**gger**, hot → ho**tter** |
| 2 sílabas terminado en y | **-ier** | happy → happ**ier**, easy → eas**ier** |
| Largo (2+ sílabas) | **more** + adj + than | **more** expensive than |

Irregulares:

| Adjetivo | Comparativo |
|---|---|
| good | better |
| bad | worse |
| far | farther / further |

- *Buenos Aires is **bigger than** Rosario.*
- *Math is **more difficult than** history for me.*

Siempre **than** (no *that*) para decir "que".

## Día 2 — Superlativos

Para decir que algo es **el más** de un grupo:

| Tipo | Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Corto | **the** + -est | the cheap**est**, the bi**ggest** |
| Terminado en y | **the** + -iest | the happ**iest** |
| Largo | **the most** + adj | **the most** expensive |
| Irregulares | | **the best**, **the worst**, **the farthest** |

- *Aconcagua is **the highest** mountain **in** America.*
- *It was **the best** day **of** my life.*

Con **lugares y grupos** se usa **in**: *the best restaurant **in** town*. Con períodos de tiempo, **of**: *the best day **of** the year*.

## Día 3 — As ... as y not as ... as

| Estructura | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| as + adj + as | tan ... como | Lucas is **as tall as** his dad. |
| not as + adj + as | no tan ... como | This hotel is **not as cheap as** the other one. |

El adjetivo queda **en su forma normal**: *as **tall** as* (nunca *as taller as*).

*Not as ... as* es una forma amable de comparar: *My English isn't as good as yours* suena más suave que *Your English is better than mine*.

## Día 4 — Contables e incontables: some y any

**Contables**: se pueden contar y tienen plural → *a chair, two chairs, an egg, three eggs*.

**Incontables**: no se cuentan de a uno y **no tienen plural** → *water, milk, money, time, music, bread, information, advice, furniture*.

Ojo con estos, que en español sí son contables: **information** (información/informaciones), **advice** (consejo/consejos), **furniture** (muebles), **news** (noticias). Nunca llevan *a/an* ni *-s*.

| | Afirmativo | Negativo | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uso | **some** | **any** | **any** |
| Contables | There are **some** apples. | There aren't **any** apples. | Are there **any** apples? |
| Incontables | There's **some** milk. | There isn't **any** milk. | Is there **any** milk? |

Excepción: en **ofrecimientos y pedidos** se usa **some** aunque sea pregunta: *Would you like **some** tea? Can I have **some** bread?*

## Día 5 — Much, many, a lot of, a few y a little

| | Contables (plural) | Incontables |
|---|---|---|
| mucho/s | **many** / a lot of | **much** / a lot of |
| unos pocos / un poco | **a few** | **a little** |
| ¿cuánto/s? | **How many...?** | **How much...?** |

- **much** y **many** suenan naturales en **negativas y preguntas**: *I don't have **much** money. Do you have **many** friends?*
- En afirmativas se prefiere **a lot of**: *She has **a lot of** books.*

> My city isn't very big, but there are a lot of things to do. There aren't many museums, but there are a few nice parks. I don't have much free time during the week, so I only go out a little. How much time do you spend outside?

## En pocas palabras

- Cortos: **-er / the -est**. Largos: **more / the most**.
- **good → better → the best**; **bad → worse → the worst**.
- **as ... as** = tan ... como.
- Incontables sin plural: *information, advice, money*.
- **some** en afirmativas y ofrecimientos; **any** en negativas y preguntas.
- **many / a few** con contables; **much / a little** con incontables.
