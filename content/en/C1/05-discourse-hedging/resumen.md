# Marcadores del discurso y atenuación

El último tópico del curso es sobre **cómo se arma un discurso**: los marcadores que ordenan un texto, los que usás al hablar para ganar tiempo o cambiar de tema, y el **hedging**, el arte de matizar lo que afirmás. Con esto vas a poder escribir un ensayo o dar una exposición con estilo académico.

## Día 1 — Marcadores para organizar un texto

| Función | Marcadores | Registro |
|---|---|---|
| Secuenciar | *First(ly), Second(ly), Finally / To begin with, Next, Lastly* | neutro |
| Agregar | *In addition, Furthermore, Moreover, What is more* | formal |
| Ejemplificar | *For instance, For example, such as* | neutro |
| Aclarar | *namely, In other words, That is to say* | formal |
| Concluir | *In conclusion, To sum up, All things considered, On balance* | formal |

- *Three factors need to be considered, **namely** cost, time and staffing.* → *Namely* = a saber.
- ***On balance**, the advantages seem to outweigh the risks.* → En términos generales...

Dos trampas:

- "Por otro lado" es ***on** the other hand* (no *in the other hand*).
- *At first* es "al principio" (*At first I hated it, but now I love it*); para enumerar, ***First*** o ***Firstly***.

## Día 2 — Marcadores de la conversación

| Para... | Marcadores |
|---|---|
| ganar tiempo | *Well..., Let me think..., How can I put it?* |
| cambiar de tema | *By the way..., Speaking of..., Anyway...* |
| volver al tema | *Going back to what you said..., As I was saying...* |
| reformular | *What I mean is..., Let me put it another way...* |
| corregir | *Actually,...* (en realidad) |
| conceder | *Admittedly,..., To be fair,...* |
| relativizar | *Mind you,...* (igual, de todos modos) |

- *The hotel was a bit noisy. **Mind you**, it was very cheap.*
- ***Actually**, I'm not from Rosario. I'm from Salta.* → En realidad...

Falsos amigos: *actually* no es "actualmente" (*currently*) y *eventually* no es "eventualmente" (*if necessary*): *eventually* = finalmente, con el tiempo.

## Día 3 — Atenuación (hedging)

En un texto académico casi nunca se afirma algo como verdad absoluta. Se **matiza**:

| Recurso | Afirmación fuerte | Afirmación matizada |
|---|---|---|
| Verbos | *This **proves** that...* | *This **suggests / indicates** that...* |
| *seem / appear* | *The method **is** effective.* | *The method **appears to be** effective.* |
| Modales | *This **is** due to...* | *This **may be** due to...* |
| *tend to* | *Prices **rise** in winter.* | *Prices **tend to** rise in winter.* |
| Cuantificadores | ***All** experts agree.* | ***Most** experts agree.* |
| Adverbios | *It **is** the best option.* | *It is **arguably** the best option.* |
| Expresiones | *Social media changed reading.* | *Social media has, **to some extent**, changed reading.* |

***It could be argued that...*** es la fórmula clásica para presentar una idea sin comprometerte del todo.

## Día 4 — Estilo académico: citar y contraargumentar

**Citar**: el verbo que elegís muestra tu postura.

| Aceptás la idea | Neutro | Tomás distancia |
|---|---|---|
| *shows, demonstrates, points out* | *argues, states, suggests* | *claims, alleges* |

- *As Smith (2019) **points out**, creativity is often undervalued in schools.*
- *Some authors **claim** that grammar teaching is unnecessary.* → No lo das por cierto.

**Contraargumentar**:

- ***While it is true that** technology can be distracting, it also offers unique opportunities.*
- ***Critics argue that** the policy is too expensive; **however**, ...*
- *This view **fails to take into account** the long-term costs.*

Para tu opinión: ***In my view*** o ***I would argue that*** (nunca *according to me*).

## Día 5 — Un ensayo breve

> ***Is remote work here to stay?***
>
> ***This essay argues that** remote work, while beneficial, is not suitable for every profession. **To begin with**, there is **considerable evidence that** working from home **tends to** improve productivity in tasks that require concentration. **As** Jones (2022) **points out**, employees **appear to** save over an hour a day on commuting.*
>
> ***Admittedly**, the evidence is limited, and **it could be argued that** the benefits depend **largely** on each person's situation. **Furthermore**, **some** jobs, **such as** nursing or construction, simply cannot be done remotely. **While it is true that** technology will continue to change the workplace, face-to-face contact **is likely to** remain essential in many sectors.*
>
> ***On balance**, **it would seem that** remote work is **here to stay**, but as one option among several rather than a universal solution.*

## En pocas palabras

- Marcadores para **ordenar** (*Firstly, Furthermore, namely, On balance*) y para **conversar** (*Anyway, Admittedly, Mind you*).
- **Hedging**: *suggest, appear to, tend to, may, most, to some extent* en vez de afirmaciones absolutas.
- Estilo académico: citá con el verbo justo (*points out / claims*) y contraargumentá con ***While it is true that...***
