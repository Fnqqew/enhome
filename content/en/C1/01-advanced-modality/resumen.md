# Modalidad avanzada

En C1 ya no alcanza con *must*, *might* y *should*: necesitás **matices**. Esta semana aprendés a distinguir lo que **no hacía falta** pero hiciste igual, a medir **qué tan probable** es algo con precisión y a expresar obligaciones y expectativas en el **registro** adecuado.

## Día 1 — Needn't have vs. didn't need to

La diferencia está en si **lo hiciste o no**:

| Forma | ¿Lo hiciste? | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **needn't have** + participio | Sí, pero no hacía falta. | *You **needn't have brought** a present.* |
| **didn't need to** + verbo | Normalmente no. | *I **didn't need to take** a taxi, so I walked.* |

- *We **needn't have hurried**: the train was late.* → Nos apuramos al vicio.
- *It was sunny, so I **didn't need to take** an umbrella.* → No lo llevé.

No lo confundas con *shouldn't have*:

- *You **needn't have** paid.* → No hacía falta que pagaras (era innecesario).
- *You **shouldn't have** paid.* → No tendrías que haber pagado (estuvo mal). Ojo: como agradecimiento, *Oh, you shouldn't have!* = ¡No te hubieras molestado!

*Needn't* suena británico; en inglés americano se usa más *didn't have to* o *didn't need to*.

## Día 2 — Be bound to, be likely to y be set to

| Expresión | Grado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **be bound to / be sure to** | casi seguro | *It's **bound to** rain on the day of the wedding.* |
| **be likely to** | probable | *Prices **are likely to** rise again.* |
| **be set to** | previsto, en camino | *The company **is set to** announce record profits.* |
| **be unlikely to** | poco probable | *The law **is unlikely to** pass before June.* |

- *Likely* es adjetivo: *He's **likely to** win* o *It's **likely that** he'll win*. En inglés americano también se usa *He'll **likely** win*.
- *Probable* no va con persona: *It's **probable that** he'll win* (no *He is probable to win*).

*Be set to* aparece mucho en titulares: *Inflation **set to** fall this year.*

## Día 3 — Matices de la especulación

| Certeza | Recurso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Muy alta | *in all likelihood, almost certainly* | ***In all likelihood**, they'll accept the offer.* |
| Alta | **may / might well** | *She **may well** be right.* |
| Alta | ***Chances are...*** | ***Chances are** she's already heard.* |
| Bastante alta | *there's a good chance* | ***There's a good chance** it'll be delayed.* |
| Posible, y fácil que pase | **could easily** | *That **could easily** happen to anyone.* |
| Imposible | **can't possibly** | *He **can't possibly** have finished already.* |

Posición de los adverbios: **después** de *will* y **antes** de *won't*:

- *She**'ll probably** come.* / *She **probably won't** come.*

## Día 4 — Obligación, permiso y expectativas

| Expresión | Matiz | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **be supposed to** | se supone que (regla o plan) | *We **were supposed to** meet at six.* |
| **had better** | más vale que (advertencia) | *You**'d better** leave now.* |
| **be required to** | obligación formal | *Passengers **are required to** wear seatbelts.* |
| **be allowed to** | permiso | *I **was allowed to** leave early.* |
| **it's up to you** | depende de vos | *Stay or go, **it's up to you**.* |
| **there's no point in** | no tiene sentido | ***There's no point in** arguing.* |

*Be supposed to* muchas veces implica que **no se cumplió**: *He **was supposed to** call me yesterday* (y no llamó).

*Had better* no es pasado ni lleva *to*: *You'd better **go*** (no *had better to go*).

## Día 5 — Un reclamo formal

> *Dear Sir or Madam,*
>
> *I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with the installation service I booked for March 3. Your technician **was supposed to** arrive between 9 a.m. and 12 p.m., but nobody turned up, and I was not informed of any delay. As it turned out, I **needn't have taken** the day off work.*
>
> *I understand that problems **can easily** occur. However, I then **had to** call your helpline four times before anyone answered. Further delays **are bound to** cause serious problems for my business, and it **is unlikely that** I will renew my contract unless the situation is resolved.*
>
> *I would therefore appreciate a new appointment within the next 48 hours and compensation for the missed day. I look forward to your prompt reply.*
>
> *Sincerely,*
> *Mariana López*

## En pocas palabras

- ***needn't have*** + participio: lo hiciste y no hacía falta; ***didn't need to***: no hacía falta (y por lo general no lo hiciste).
- ***bound to*** (casi seguro) > ***likely to*** (probable) > ***might*** > ***unlikely to***; ***set to*** = previsto.
- ***be supposed to*** = se supone que; ***had better*** = más vale que; ***be required to*** = obligación formal.
