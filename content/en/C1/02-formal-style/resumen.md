# Nominalización y estilo formal

El inglés oral se apoya en **verbos** (*prices went up fast*); el inglés formal, en **sustantivos** (*a rapid increase in prices*). Esta semana aprendés a hacer ese cambio, a elegir vocabulario de **registro alto** y a escribir con un tono **objetivo**, como en un informe, un paper o una carta institucional.

## Día 1 — Qué es la nominalización

**Nominalizar** es convertir una acción (verbo) o una cualidad (adjetivo) en un **sustantivo**.

| Sufijo | Verbo o adjetivo | Sustantivo |
|---|---|---|
| **-tion / -sion** | *reduce, decide* | *reduction, decision* |
| **-ment** | *develop, improve* | *development, improvement* |
| **-ance / -ence** | *perform, depend* | *performance, dependence* |
| **-ity** | *possible, available* | *possibility, availability* |
| **-al** | *refuse, approve* | *refusal, approval* |
| **-ness** | *aware, effective* | *awareness, effectiveness* |
| **-ure** | *fail, close* | *failure, closure* |

Algunas palabras no cambian: *increase, change, decline, rise, demand, use*.

- *Prices **increased rapidly**.* → *There was a **rapid increase** in prices.*
- *The project **failed** because they **planned** badly.* → *The **failure** of the project was due to poor **planning**.*

## Día 2 — Del verbo al sustantivo: transformar frases

Al nominalizar cambian varias piezas a la vez:

| Oración verbal | Frase nominal |
|---|---|
| verbo: *expanded* | sustantivo: *expansion* |
| adverbio: *quickly* | adjetivo: ***rapid** expansion* |
| sujeto: *the company* | posesivo o *of*: *the company's / of the company* |

- *The company expanded quickly.* → *The company's **rapid expansion**...*

El sustantivo necesita **verbos que lo sostengan** y **preposiciones fijas**:

| Verbo + sustantivo | Sustantivo + preposición |
|---|---|
| *make a decision* | *an increase **in*** |
| *carry out research* | *demand **for*** |
| *reach an agreement* | *an impact / effect **on*** |
| *give rise to* | *a solution **to***, *access **to*** |
| *take into account* | *an investigation **into*** |

Ojo con el exceso: *the implementation of the improvement of the evaluation process* es correcto, pero ilegible.

## Día 3 — Vocabulario formal

Muchos phrasal verbs tienen un equivalente **latino**, que para vos es una ventaja porque se parece al español:

| Informal | Formal |
|---|---|
| *find out* | *discover, establish* |
| *put off* | *postpone* |
| *go up / go down* | *increase / decrease* |
| *look into* | *investigate, examine* |
| *get* | *obtain, receive* |
| *a lot of* | *a considerable number / amount of* |
| *need / help* | *require / assist* |

Además: **sin contracciones** (*do not*, *it is*) y sin palabras vagas como *thing, stuff, really, kids*.

Pero cuidado con los **falsos amigos**: *solicit* no es "solicitar" (es *request*), *discuss* no lleva *about* y *actually* no es "actualmente".

## Día 4 — Estructuras impersonales y objetividad

| Recurso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| *It* + pasiva | ***It is widely acknowledged that** early education has long-term benefits.* |
| *It* + modal + pasiva | ***It could be argued that** the policy has failed.* |
| *There* + *be* | ***There appears to be** a correlation between sleep and performance.* |
| Sujeto abstracto | ***The results suggest** that the method is effective.* |
| Frase participial | ***Having reviewed** the evidence, the panel recommended a change.* |

Con las frases participiales, el sujeto principal tiene que ser **quien hizo la acción**:

- *Having finished the report, **I** sent it.* ✔️
- *Having finished the report, **the computer** crashed.* ❌ (¿la computadora terminó el informe?)

## Día 5 — De informal a formal

Notas informales de una reunión:

> *Lots of people complained about the schedule, so we changed it. We also let people work flexible hours, and now everyone's much happier. People don't miss work as much, either.*

El mismo contenido en un informe:

> ***Following a large number of complaints**, the schedule **was revised**. **In addition**, flexible working hours **were introduced**. **The introduction of** flexible hours **has resulted in** considerably **higher staff satisfaction**. **Furthermore**, a significant **reduction in absenteeism** has been observed.*

Fijate en los pasos: acciones → sustantivos (*complained → complaints, introduced → introduction*), conectores formales y sujetos abstractos.

## En pocas palabras

- **Nominalizar**: *decide → decision, improve → improvement*; con su preposición fija (*increase in, impact on*).
- Registro formal: verbos latinos, sin contracciones ni palabras vagas.
- Tono objetivo con ***It is ... that***, ***There appears to be*** y sujetos abstractos.
