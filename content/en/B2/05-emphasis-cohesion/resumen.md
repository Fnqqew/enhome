# Énfasis, inversión y cohesión

Cerramos B2 con recursos para escribir textos con más fuerza y mejor hilados. En español resaltamos cosas cambiando el orden casi con libertad ("fue mi hermana la que..."); en inglés el orden es más rígido, así que hay **estructuras específicas** para dar énfasis. Y para que un texto fluya, necesitás **conectores** variados y bien usados.

## Día 1 — Oraciones escindidas: It is… that y What… is

| Estructura | Ejemplo | En español |
|---|---|---|
| **It is / was ... that / who** | *It was **Ana** who called, not Pablo.* | Fue Ana la que llamó. |
| **What ... is / was** | *What I need **is a vacation**.* | Lo que necesito es... |
| **What ... do / did is / was** | *What I did **was call** the police.* | Lo que hice fue... |
| **All ... is** | *All I want **is some sleep**.* | Lo único que quiero es... |
| **The reason why ... is** | *The reason why I left **is** simple.* | El motivo por el que... |

Con *It was ... that* podés destacar cualquier parte:

- *It was **in 2019** that we met.* → Fue en 2019 cuando nos conocimos.
- *It's **not the money** that worries me.* → No es la plata lo que me preocupa.

Nunca te olvides del *It* inicial: *It was my brother...* (no *Was my brother...*).

## Día 2 — Inversión después de expresiones negativas

Si una oración empieza con una expresión **negativa o restrictiva**, el sujeto y el auxiliar se invierten, **como en una pregunta**.

| Expresión | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| **Never** | *Never **have I** seen such a mess.* |
| **Rarely / Seldom** | *Rarely **do we** get such good news.* |
| **Not only ... but also** | *Not only **did she** win, but she also broke the record.* |
| **No sooner ... than** | *No sooner **had we** sat down than the phone rang.* |
| **Hardly ... when** | *Hardly **had I** arrived when it started to rain.* |
| **Under no circumstances** | *Under no circumstances **should you** open this door.* |
| **Not until / Only when** | *Not until I got home **did I** realize I'd lost my keys.* |

Si no hay auxiliar, agregás **do / does / did**. Es un recurso **formal o literario**: en la charla decís *I've never seen such a mess*.

## Día 3 — Conectores de contraste

| Conector | Qué va después | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **although / even though** | oración | *Even though I was exhausted, I kept going.* |
| **whereas / while** | oración (dos hechos opuestos) | *I love the beach, **whereas** my wife prefers the mountains.* |
| **despite / in spite of** | sustantivo, *-ing*, *the fact that* | ***Despite having** little experience, she got the job.* |
| **however / nevertheless** | nueva oración, con coma | *It's expensive. **Nevertheless**, it's our best option.* |
| **on the other hand** | el otro lado de algo | *It's small. **On the other hand**, it's very central.* |
| **on the contrary** | niega lo anterior | *It wasn't boring. **On the contrary**, it was fascinating.* |

La trampa: "por el contrario" muchas veces se traduce *on the other hand*, no *on the contrary*.

## Día 4 — Causa y resultado

| Tipo | Conectores | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| Causa + oración | *because, as, since* | ***As** it was late, we took a taxi.* |
| Causa + sustantivo | *because of, due to, owing to* | *The flight was canceled **due to** the fog.* |
| Resultado | *so, therefore, consequently, as a result* | *Sales fell. **As a result**, several stores closed.* |
| Intensidad + resultado | *so + adjetivo + that, such (a) + sustantivo + that* | *It was **such a** boring movie **that** I fell asleep.* |

*Due to* y *because of* nunca van seguidos de una oración: *due to **the snow*** (no *due to it was snowing*).

## Día 5 — Un artículo de opinión

> ***Should cities ban cars from downtown?***
>
> ***What many people fail to realize** is that our downtown areas were not designed for cars. Every day, thousands of vehicles fill the streets and, **as a result**, the air we breathe is getting worse. **Not only does** a car-free center reduce pollution, **but it also** makes the streets safer for everyone.*
>
> ***Despite** these benefits, some shop owners are worried. They argue that fewer cars will mean fewer customers, **whereas** studies from other cities show the opposite. **It is** pedestrians and cyclists, not drivers, **who** spend more time in local stores.*
>
> ***All in all**, the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks. **Therefore**, I believe our city should take the first step.*

## En pocas palabras

- ***It was ... that*** y ***What ... is*** para destacar información.
- Expresión negativa al principio → **auxiliar + sujeto**: *Never have I...*
- *Despite / due to* + sustantivo; *although / because* + oración.
