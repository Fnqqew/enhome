# Deseos, lamentos y verbos de reporte

Esta semana tiene dos partes. En la primera aprendés a decir **"ojalá"**, **"me arrepiento"**, **"prefiero que..."** y **"ya es hora de que..."**, estructuras donde el inglés usa un **pasado** para hablar de algo irreal. En la segunda, los **verbos de reporte**: en lugar de repetir *he said*, resumís la intención (*he suggested, she admitted, they warned us*).

## Día 1 — Wish e if only

| Deseo sobre... | Estructura | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| el presente | **wish + past simple** | *I wish I **had** more free time.* |
| el pasado (lamento) | **wish + past perfect** | *I wish I **hadn't said** that.* |
| una capacidad | **wish + could** | *I wish I **could** speak Chinese.* |

- *I wish I **were** taller.* → Ojalá fuera más alto. (En la charla también se oye *was*.)
- ***If only** we **had left** earlier!* → ¡Si tan solo hubiéramos salido antes! Es igual que *wish*, pero más intenso.

¿*Wish* o *hope*? *Hope* es para lo **posible**; *wish*, para lo **irreal**:

- *I **hope** you pass.* → Ojalá apruebes (puede pasar).
- *I **wish** I had studied.* → Ojalá hubiera estudiado (ya no se puede cambiar).

## Día 2 — Wish + would, would rather e it's time

| Estructura | Uso | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **wish + persona + would** | queja sobre lo que hace otro | *I wish you **would** stop interrupting me.* |
| **would rather + verbo** | preferencia propia | *I**'d rather stay** home tonight.* |
| **would rather + persona + pasado** | preferencia sobre otro | *I'd rather you **didn't** smoke here.* |
| **it's time + sujeto + pasado** | ya es hora de que | *It's time we **left**.* |
| **it's time + to + verbo** | ya es hora de | *It's time **to go**.* |

Con *high* o *about* suena más urgente o impaciente: *It's **high time** you **got** a job.* → Ya va siendo hora de que consigas trabajo.

Ojo: *wish + would* es para **otra persona** o cosa; no se dice *I wish I would*.

## Día 3 — Suggest, admit y deny

Estos verbos van con **-ing** o con **that**, nunca con *persona + to*:

| Verbo | Con -ing | Con that |
|---|---|---|
| **suggest** | *He suggested **going** out.* | *He suggested **that we go** out.* |
| **admit** | *She admitted **lying**.* | *She admitted **that she had lied**.* |
| **deny** | *He denied **taking** it.* | *He denied **that he had taken** it.* |

Con *having + participio* se marca que la acción fue antes: *He denied **having seen** her.*

Verbos con preposición + *-ing*:

- ***apologize for**: She apologized for being late.*
- ***insist on**: He insisted on paying.*
- ***accuse someone of**: They accused him of cheating.*

## Día 4 — Advise, warn, remind y otros

| Patrón | Verbos | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| persona + **(not) to** + verbo | *advise, warn, remind, persuade, encourage, invite* | *She **warned us not to touch** it.* |
| **to** + verbo, sin persona | *offer, promise, refuse, agree, threaten* | *He **offered to drive** me home.* |

El verbo de reporte **resume** lo que alguien quiso hacer:

| Lo que dijo | Cómo lo reportás |
|---|---|
| *"Don't forget your passport!"* | *She **reminded me to take** my passport.* |
| *"I'll pay you back on Friday."* | *He **promised to pay** me back on Friday.* |
| *"I won't answer that."* | *She **refused to answer**.* |
| *"Shall I carry your bag?"* | *He **offered to carry** my bag.* |

## Día 5 — Un resumen de reunión

> ***Meeting summary – Marketing team, May 12***
>
> *Martín opened the meeting and **suggested postponing** the launch until March. The sales team **admitted that** the targets **had been** too ambitious, but Sofía **pointed out that** the new campaign was already working. Laura **warned us not to underestimate** the competition and **insisted on doing** more research before deciding. Pablo **offered to prepare** a new budget.*
>
> *In the end, everybody **agreed to postpone** the launch. Several people said they **wished** they **had had** more time to prepare, and Martín added that it was **high time** the team **planned** further ahead. The next meeting will be held on May 26.*

## En pocas palabras

- **wish + pasado** (presente irreal), **wish + past perfect** (lamento); *hope* para lo posible.
- **would rather** e **it's time** + persona + pasado: *I'd rather you didn't... / It's time we left.*
- **suggest / admit / deny + -ing o that**; **advise / warn / remind + persona + (not) to**.
