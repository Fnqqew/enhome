# Can y presente continuo

Esta semana sumás dos herramientas: **can**, para hablar de lo que podés o sabés hacer, y el **presente continuo**, para lo que está pasando **ahora**.

## Día 1 — Can: habilidad y permiso

**Can** es un verbo especial (modal): es **igual para todas las personas** y va seguido del **verbo base, sin to**.

| Afirmativo | Negativo | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|
| I can swim. | I can't swim. | Can you swim? |
| She can drive. | She can't drive. | Can she drive? |

Respuestas cortas: *Yes, I can.* / *No, I can't.*

Usos:

- **Habilidad** (saber, poder): *I **can** play the piano.* → Sé tocar el piano.
- **Permiso**: ***Can** I use your phone?* → ¿Puedo usar tu teléfono?
- **Pedidos**: ***Can** you help me?* → ¿Me ayudás?

Nunca: ~~she cans~~, ~~can to go~~.

## Día 2 — Presente continuo: cómo se forma

Estructura: **am / is / are + verbo-ing**.

| | Afirmativo | Negativo | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I'm eating | I'm not eating | Am I eating? |
| he / she / it | she's eating | she isn't eating | Is she eating? |
| you / we / they | they're eating | they aren't eating | Are they eating? |

Reglas del **-ing**:

| Regla | Ejemplo |
|---|---|
| General: + ing | read → read**ing** |
| Termina en **e** muda: se va la e | make → mak**ing**, write → writ**ing** |
| Consonante-vocal-consonante (una sílaba): se duplica | run → run**ning**, sit → sit**ting** |
| Termina en **ie**: cambia a y | lie → l**ying**, die → d**ying** |

## Día 3 — Presente continuo: cuándo se usa

1. **Algo que pasa en este momento**: *Be quiet! The baby **is sleeping**.*
2. **Algo temporal**, aunque no sea justo ahora: *I**'m reading** a great book this month.*

Palabras que suelen acompañarlo: *now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, Look!, Listen!*

**Verbos de estado.** Algunos verbos expresan estados, no acciones, y casi nunca van en continuo:

*know, understand, like, love, hate, want, need, believe, remember*

- *I **know** the answer* ✔️ — *I'm knowing* ❌
- *She **wants** a coffee* ✔️ — *She's wanting* ❌

## Día 4 — Presente simple vs. continuo

| Presente simple | Presente continuo |
|---|---|
| Hábitos, rutinas, verdades generales | Lo que pasa ahora o es temporal |
| *I **work** in an office.* | *I**'m working** from home today.* |
| *She **plays** tennis on Sundays.* | *She**'s playing** tennis right now.* |
| always, usually, every day, never | now, at the moment, today, this week |

Combinación típica:

> Carlos **usually takes** the bus to work, but today he**'s riding** his bike because the buses **aren't running**.

## Día 5 — Describir una escena

Para describir una foto o un lugar combinás todo lo aprendido:

- **There is / there are** para decir qué hay.
- **Presente continuo** para decir qué están haciendo.
- **Preposiciones** para ubicar: *on the left, on the right, in the middle, in the background, next to, behind*.

> In this picture, there's a park. On the left, there are two children. They're playing with a ball. In the middle, a woman is sitting on a bench and she's reading. Behind her, a dog is running. It's a sunny day and everybody is having fun.

## En pocas palabras

- **Can + verbo base** para habilidad, permiso y pedidos. Nunca *to* ni *-s*.
- Presente continuo: **am/is/are + -ing**.
- Simple para **hábitos**; continuo para **ahora** y lo **temporal**.
- Los verbos de estado (*know, like, want*) van en presente simple.
